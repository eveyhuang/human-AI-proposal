Embedding Space Visualization

UMAP 2D Projection

92 proposals projected from 1024-dim to 2D. X markers = group centroids. Magenta circles = outliers.

GPT-5.2 forms a tight, isolated cluster on the far left, visually confirming near-identical output.

Claude proposals are spread across multiple regions, forming distinct subclusters.

Gemini shows moderate clustering with some spread.

Human proposals are distributed throughout the center-right, overlapping with Claude and Gemini.

Outliers (magenta) are predominantly human and GPT-5.2 proposals at the space periphery.

t-SNE projection (not shown) confirms same clustering patterns, reinforcing structural validity of findings.
