Key Findings

GPT-5.2 paradox: most outliers despite least diverse

26.1% outliers. Its extreme internal similarity creates a tight, isolated cluster far from everyone else. Individual proposals appear as outliers in the global space.

Claude: zero outliers despite highest diversity

0% outliers, smallest NN distances (mean 0.014). Claude forms subclusters that are individually tight but spread across the space. Each proposal has a close neighbor.

Human proposals produce genuine outliers

13% outliers. 43.5% have NN from own group; 56.5% NN from AI. Humans occupy shared territory but also produce truly unique ideas.

100% of AI proposals' nearest neighbors are other AI

No AI proposal is closest to a human proposal. 95.7% of Claude's NN are from Claude itself. Suggests systematic framing differences between AI and human approaches.
