Analysis 2: Centroid Dispersion

Results

Mean distance from each proposal to its group centroid. Larger = more spread out from center.

Key Findings

Claude shows massive dispersion from its centroid

Mean dist: 0.179 vs Human 0.032. Cliff's Delta = +0.99 (near-maximum large effect, p < 0.001). Proposals spread widely around center.

GPT-5.2 is tightly clustered around its centroid

Mean dist: 0.010. Cliff's Delta = -1.00 (p < 0.001). Variance: 0.000005 vs Human 0.00008. Near-zero spread: all proposals essentially the same point in embedding space.

Gemini is less dispersed than humans

Mean dist: 0.047. Cliff's Delta = -0.82 (large effect, p < 0.001). Moderate clustering, though has some high-distance outlier proposals.

All AI combined vs Human: not significant

Cliff's Delta = -0.17, p = 0.22. Claude's high dispersion offsets GPT-5.2's low dispersion, producing a misleading null result when aggregated.

Consistency: Confirms pairwise analysis ranking: Claude > Human > Gemini > GPT-5.2
