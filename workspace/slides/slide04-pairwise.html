Analysis 1: Within-Group Pairwise Diversity

Results

Cliff's Delta effect sizes vs Human baseline. Positive = AI more diverse. *** p < 0.001

Key Findings

Claude Opus 4.5 is the only model MORE diverse than humans

Mean pairwise distance: 0.340 vs Human 0.066 (small effect, p < 0.001). However, highly skewed distribution: median 0.072 with long tail to 0.8.

GPT-5.2 shows extreme homogeneity

Mean pairwise distance: 0.022 (3x lower than humans). Cliff's Delta = -1.00 (maximum possible effect). Proposals are near-identical.

Gemini 3 Pro is moderately less diverse than humans

Mean: 0.096. Cliff's Delta = -0.73 (large effect, p < 0.001). More varied than GPT-5.2 but still significantly less than human.

All AI combined shows a small negative effect

Cliff's Delta = -0.29 (small). Aggregating masks dramatic between-model differences.
