Methodology

Embedding Pipeline

1. BioLinkBERT-large

340M param biomedical model pretrained on PubMed with citation links. SOTA on biomedical NLP.

2. Overlapping Chunk Averaging

400-word chunks, 50-word overlap. Averaged to one 1024-dim vector per proposal. Uses 100% of text.

3. Cosine Distance

All diversity metrics computed as cosine distance in 1024-dim embedding space.

Statistical Tests

Mann-Whitney U

Cliff's Delta

Permutation (10K)

Three Complementary Analyses

Pairwise Diversity

Within-group cosine distances between all pairs. Measures how different proposals are from each other.

Centroid Dispersion

Distance from each proposal to its group centroid. Measures spread around central tendency.

Nearest-Neighbor Outlier Detection

NN distance in combined space (92 proposals). Top 10% = outliers. Tracks which group NN belongs to.

Consistent findings across all three analyses strengthens conclusions.
