Implications & Limitations

What This Means

AI models are not interchangeable for scientific ideation

The range from GPT-5.2's mode collapse to Claude's subcluster diversity shows that model choice drastically affects the space of ideas generated.

AI-generated proposals occupy a distinct region from humans

100% of AI nearest neighbors are other AI. Even the most diverse AI model (Claude) does not fully overlap with human idea space, suggesting systematic framing differences.

Human proposals contribute unique outlier ideas

13% of human proposals occupy territory far from both human and AI clusters. These "edge of the idea space" proposals represent novel thinking that AI may not replicate.

Aggregated comparisons mask critical model differences

Combined "AI vs Human" comparisons show small or null effects because Claude's diversity offsets GPT-5.2's homogeneity. Per-model analysis is essential.

Limitations: Single funding call (NCEMS); embedding similarity does not capture all dimensions of scientific novelty (e.g., feasibility, interdisciplinarity); 23 proposals per group; single generation per prompt per model; BioLinkBERT may not capture all domain-specific semantic distinctions.
