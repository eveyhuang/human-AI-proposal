Background & Motivation

Why Use AI Reviewers to Assess Novelty?

Human peer review is expensive, slow, and subject to its own biases — AI reviewers offer scalability, but introduce new risks

The Core Question

When AI models write research proposals, do they produce genuinely novel ideas — or do they recycle familiar combinations? And when AI models evaluate those proposals, are their assessments trustworthy?

Why Novelty Specifically?

Novelty is the most contested dimension in proposal review — it is subjective, multi-faceted, and hard to operationalize consistently. If AI evaluators can assess it reliably (and without bias), they become useful proxies for human expert panels.

What This Analysis Does

  • Three AI models act as reviewers, scoring proposals on 6 novelty criteria
  • Scores are compared across author groups (human, Claude, Gemini, GPT-5.2)
  • We test whether evaluators are systematically biased — especially whether they favor their own proposals
  • We correct for self-preference using a cross-evaluator design

Style-Controlled Condition

All 92 proposals were standardized (rephrased into a uniform 5-section template) before review. This ensures any score differences reflect what ideas are proposed, not how well they are written.
