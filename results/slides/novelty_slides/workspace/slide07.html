The three evaluators assign systematically different scores. The spread between the most lenient (Gemini, 4.18) and most strict (Claude, 3.18) is 1.0 points — larger than the entire human-GPT author group difference.

⚠️ Why This Matters

If one evaluator reviews more proposals from a given author group, scores for that group will be artificially inflated. Any comparison between author groups using pooled scores must account for evaluator leniency differences.
