Method · Novelty Evaluation Framework

Six Dimensions of Novelty — Grounded in Research Literature

Each proposal is scored 1–5 on six independently validated dimensions of originality

1

New Question, Topic, or Problem Framing

Does the proposal introduce a new question, topic, or way of framing the problem — an understudied area?

Guetzkow et al., 2004

2

New Theory, Concept, Method, Dataset, or Design

Does the proposal use or develop a new theory, concept, method, dataset, or research design?

Guetzkow et al., 2004; ERC, 2021

3

Unusual Combination of Existing Ideas

Does the proposal combine existing ideas, methods, or perspectives in an atypical way?

Uzzi et al., 2013

4

Beyond the State of the Art

Is the proposal ambitious — going beyond incremental extension of prior work?

ERC, 2021

5

Credible High-Risk / High-Gain Potential

Does the proposal contain a credible high-risk, high-gain element with substantial payoff if successful?

ERC, 2021

6

Unique Knowledge Generation

Could the proposal generate knowledge that would be difficult to obtain from prior work alone?

Shibayama & Wang, 2020

1–5 Scale

Scoring: 1 = Very weak evidence · 3 = Moderate / mixed evidence · 5 = Very strong evidence. Reviewers must cite specific evidence from the proposal text — vague claims of novelty do not earn high scores.
