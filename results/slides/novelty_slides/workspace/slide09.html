Bias Is Consistent Across All Criteria

GPT-5.2's self-inflation appears on all six criteria, strongest on "Unique Knowledge Generation" (+0.72) and "Beyond State of the Art" (+0.67). This is a systematic evaluative stance, not a criterion-specific quirk.

Claude's Self-Deprecation Is Equally Systematic

Claude is consistently harder on its own proposals across all dimensions. Its strictest criterion is "Unique Knowledge Generation" (−0.60) — the very one where GPT most strongly inflates. Opposite biases amplify the apparent GPT–Claude gap.

Key Implication

These cross-evaluator asymmetries inflate the GPT–Claude gap in any pooled analysis. Removing self-evaluations is essential before comparing author groups.
